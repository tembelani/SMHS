Design Normalization

The database schema is designed to manage, sort and handle housing rentals in a normalized manner.
The four entities are Students, Buildings, Rooms and Contracts which are essential for this system
to operate. Primary keys are highlighted and identified. Foreign keys are established as well. 
Rooms belong to Buildings and Contracts are signed by Students and linked to specific rooms. 

Normalization was applied:
> 1NF - all attributes are atomic (e.g., studentID per student)
> 2NF - non-key attributes depend fully on the PK
> 3NF - transitive dependencies were removed (e.g., Building manager is stored in Buildings, not Rooms)

Constraints:
> Student - can't exceed their max_budget
> Rooms - can't have invalid capacities/rents
> Contracts - must have valid start_date, end_date, room_id and unique constraint which prevents double booking of room

This design is scalable and supports future expansion.
